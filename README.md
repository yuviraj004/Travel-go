# Travel Go – Explore the World

A modern, luxurious, and fully responsive travel and tourism platform designed to inspire wanderlust and deliver seamless trip discovery and booking experiences.

## 🌟 Brand Identity
- **Brand Name**: Travel Go
- **Tagline**: Explore the World
- **Theme Palette**: Sky Blue (`#0284c7`), Ocean Blue (`#0c4a6e`), Pure White, and Vibrant Sunset Orange (`#f97316`) accents.
- **Typography**: Plus Jakarta Sans, Outfit, and Playfair Display (editorial luxury).

## 🚀 Key Features & Pages
1. **Homepage**:
   - Sticky glassmorphic navigation bar with logo, links, dynamic multi-currency selector (USD, EUR, GBP, INR), wishlist counter, and "Book Your Trip" CTA.
   - Full-screen animated Hero section with auto-rotating slides, value props, statistics, and an interactive 4-part travel search bar.
   - **Popular Destinations**: 8 world-class destinations (Bali, Paris, Switzerland, Dubai, Maldives, Tokyo, Manali, Kashmir) with pricing, ratings, and instant inquiry.
   - **Featured Travel Packages**: All-inclusive itineraries with duration badges, inclusion chips, and direct booking actions.
   - **Why Choose Travel Go**: 6 foundational pillars and global statistics.
   - **Explore Travel Experiences**: Beach Holidays, Mountain Adventures, Luxury Escapes, Cultural Exploration, Wildlife Adventures, Romantic Getaways.
   - **Travel Inspiration**: Editorial travel magazine articles.
   - **Testimonials**: Authentic reviews from global explorers.
   - **Newsletter**: Instant subscription with welcome promo code (`EXPLORE15`).
   - **Footer**: Brand story, quick links, emergency contact info, and copyright.
2. **About Us Page**:
   - Brand heritage and travel philosophy.
   - Core guiding values (Authenticity, Excellence, Sustainability, Care).
   - Leadership & curator profiles.
   - Sustainability pledge.
3. **Destinations Directory Page**:
   - Search by keyword, filter by travel style, sort by popularity or price.
4. **Destination Details Page**:
   - In-depth overview, photo gallery, highlights, top attractions, local etiquette tips, and interactive custom inquiry sidebar.
5. **Travel Packages Directory Page**:
   - Comprehensive catalog with multi-factor filters (Style, Duration, Price, Rating).
6. **Package Details Page**:
   - Interactive Day-by-Day itinerary accordion, inclusions/exclusions breakdown, customer review criteria, and sticky real-time booking calculation widget with optional add-ons.
7. **Travel Blog / Magazine Page**:
   - Editorial cover story, category filters, and interactive read modal.
8. **Contact Us Page**:
   - Interactive contact form with validation and simulated confirmation ticket.
   - 4 Global regional offices (New York, London, Dubai, Mumbai) and 24/7 emergency hotline.
   - Frequently Asked Questions accordion.

## 🛠️ Interactive Modals & Drawers
- **Booking Checkout Modal**: Multi-step checkout with package summary, date selection, traveler details, special requests, and downloadable booking receipt.
- **Trip Planner Wizard**: Step-by-step custom itinerary builder (Destination -> Style -> Budget -> Contact).
- **Wishlist Slide-Out Drawer**: Persistent saved destinations and packages with quick book shortcuts.
- **Live Currency Converter**: Real-time conversion across all packages in USD, EUR, GBP, and INR.
- **Toast Notifications**: Feedback for favoriting, subscribing, submitting inquiries, and booking.
